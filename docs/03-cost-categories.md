# Cost Categories

## 1. Recruitment and Pre-Deployment

- Recruitment advertising
- Application processing
- Screening
- Interviews
- Background/reference checks
- Selection
- Contracts and onboarding
- Community/LGA preparation
- Stakeholder engagement

## 2. Training

- Trainers/facilitators
- Training venue
- Training materials
- Participant transport
- Meals
- Accommodation where required
- Practical training materials
- Assessment
- Certification

## 3. Equipment and Deployment

- Tablet/device
- Vital-sign equipment
- Basic home-care kit
- PPE
- Uniform
- Identification
- Device configuration
- SIM/data setup
- Deployment transport
- Initial patient enrolment

## 4. Recurring Service Delivery

- CHEW remuneration
- CHEW logistics
- Transport
- Communication/data
- Patient-care consumables
- Equipment maintenance
- Device replacement reserve
- Referral coordination

## 5. Clinical Supervision and Quality

- Clinical supervision
- Doctor oversight
- Nurse/clinical supervisor
- Case review
- Teleconsultation
- Field supervision
- Clinical audit
- Incident management
- Complaints management
- Patient satisfaction

## 6. Monitoring and Evaluation

- Baseline assessment
- Routine monitoring
- Data quality
- Analysis
- Reporting
- Quarterly impact reporting

## 7. Technology

- Cloud hosting
- Platform maintenance
- Technical support
- Data storage
- Cybersecurity
- Backups
- Device support

## 8. Corporate / Shared Operations

- Management
- HR
- Finance
- Administration
- Office
- Utilities
- Internet
- Legal
- Accounting
- Audit
- Insurance
- Banking/payment charges

## 9. Business Development

- Corporate partnership development
- Hospital partnership development
- Insurance partnership development
- Pharma partnership development
- Sales/customer acquisition
- Partnership meetings
- Proposal development
- Marketing/communications

## 10. Contingency

- Unexpected operational costs
- Inflation
- Emergency replacement
- Unplanned field expenses

Contingency should be separately identified rather than used as a general miscellaneous category.
