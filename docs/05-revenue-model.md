# Revenue Model

## Primary Revenue Stream

The first version of the model focuses on patient subscription revenue.

## Core Formula

Patients = CHEWs × Patients per CHEW

Monthly Subscription Revenue = Patients × Subscription Price per Patient

Annual Subscription Revenue = Monthly Subscription Revenue × 12

## Example

If:

- 10 CHEWs
- 10 patients per CHEW
- Subscription = ₦X per patient/month

Then:

Patients = 100

Monthly revenue = 100 × ₦X

Annual revenue = 100 × ₦X × 12

## Revenue Scenarios

The dashboard should allow the subscription price to be changed dynamically.

Suggested test points:

- ₦20,000
- ₦25,000
- ₦30,000
- ₦35,000
- ₦40,000
- ₦50,000

These are modelling scenarios and are not established EasePal Care prices.

## Future Revenue Streams

The architecture may later accommodate:

- Corporate subscriptions
- Hospital retainership
- Insurance contracts
- Pharma-supported programmes
- Government/co-funding

These are outside the first simple calculator unless specifically added later.
