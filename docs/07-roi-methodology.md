# ROI Methodology

## Purpose

The first version will not calculate a formal investor IRR unless sufficient validated financial information is available.

It will first calculate basic operating contribution.

## Core Metrics

### Revenue

Monthly Subscription Revenue

Annual Subscription Revenue

### Cost

Initial Setup Cost

Monthly Operating Cost

Annual Operating Cost

### Contribution

Monthly Contribution = Monthly Revenue - Monthly Operating Cost

Annual Contribution = Annual Revenue - Annual Operating Cost

## Future Investor Metrics

Once validated capital requirements and cash flows are available, the model may calculate:

- Break-even
- Payback period
- Return on Investment (ROI)
- Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
- Net Present Value (NPV)

## Principle

Investor returns should not be claimed from unvalidated assumptions.

The first objective is to establish reliable unit economics and scaling economics.
